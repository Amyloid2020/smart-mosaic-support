Privacy Policy

Effective: August 9, 2026 / Last updated: September 20, 2026

SMART MOSAIC does not upload the photos or videos you select, detection results, or exported media to a developer-operated server. Media processing is performed on your device.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the SMART MOSAIC iOS and macOS apps (“the App”).

2. Media processed on your device

The App accesses only the photos or videos you explicitly select. Face detection, license plate detection, tracking, manual selections, and mosaic rendering are performed on the device. We do not send or store your media, thumbnails, detected locations, or exported results on our servers.

3. Advertising and consent

The macOS app is ad-free in both the free and Pro versions. It does not include advertising SDKs or use advertising tracking.

The free iOS version may use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy choices where required. Depending on your choices, applicable law, and device settings, Google and its advertising partners may process an advertising identifier, device information, approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostics. The App does not provide your photos or videos to the advertising SDK.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. The App checks purchase status to provide Pro features. On macOS, Pro removes the video export duration limit and watermark. On iOS, Pro also removes ads.

5. Media and file access

On macOS, the App accesses the video files and export locations you choose in the file dialogs.

On iOS, with your permission, the App accesses selected media and saves edited results. Your original files are not modified.

6. Retention and deletion

Because media is not collected on our servers, there is no server-side media for us to delete. You control original and exported files on your device. On iOS, advertising privacy choices can be changed from the App’s privacy settings.

7. Security

SMART MOSAIC uses on-device media processing, minimal permissions, and Apple platform security controls to protect your privacy.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when features, SDKs, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be announced on this page or in the App.

10. Contact

For privacy or product questions, visit the SMART MOSAIC support page.
